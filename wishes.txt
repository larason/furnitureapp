Yes, you can absolutely do this in Tanzania. You do not need to deal with the complexity or expense of creating multiple separate companies just to run different business lines. [1, 2, 3] 
Under the Tanzanian Companies Act and the Business Registrations and Licensing Agency (BRELA) system, a single private limited company (e.g., Apex Company Limited) is legally allowed to operate multiple completely unrelated business activities under one roof. [1, 2] 
This single-company setup requires a specific drafting strategy for your MEMARTS and specific post-incorporation licensing steps:
## 1. How to Structure the MEMARTS (Objectives Clause)
When filling out the registration application on BRELA's Online Services platform, your customized Memorandum of Association (MEMARTS) must have an "Objects Clause" divided into clear paragraphs. You will list them all as "Main Objectives": [1, 4, 5] 

* 
* Objective A (Furniture): "To carry on the business of designing, manufacturing, assembling, branding, distributing, wholesaling, and retailing all types of home and office furniture, fixtures, and interior decor products."
* Objective B (Software & IT): "To engage in the business of information technology services, including but not limited to software engineering, mobile application development, IT consulting, cloud computing, and hardware maintenance."
* Objective C (Advertising): "To establish and operate an advertising, media, and marketing agency, including the creation, production, publishing, and digital broadcasting of promotional materials and commercial campaigns."
* 

## 2. Handling the Names (Trading as / Business Names)
Since you are using one company, you only have one legal entity (Apex Company Limited). However, if you don't want your software clients or fashion customers seeing "Apex Furniture" on everything, [BRELA](https://bos.brela.go.tz/) allows you to register Business Names (Trading Names) owned by the company.

* 
* You can register a business name like "Apex Digital" or "Apex Furnishings".
* In the BRELA system, the owner of these business names will not be you as an individual; the owner will be Apex Company Limited. [6] 
* 

## 3. The Catch: The "Business License" Requirement ⚠️
While BRELA allows you to put 20 different business activities into one MEMARTS, you cannot use a single Business License for all of them. [1, 7] 
Tanzanian regulatory bodies and Municipal Councils issue licenses per activity and location. Under your single company, you will have to obtain: [7, 8] 

   1. A Municipal Business License for furniture manufacturing/retail.
   2. An IT/Software Professional Service License (usually from the Ministry of Industry or Municipal depending on exact scope).
   3. An Advertising License (regulated by the Ministry of Information, Communication, and Information Technology or TCRA depending on the media platforms used). [7] 

## Summary: Single Entity vs. Multi-Entity Setup

* 
* The Big Benefit: You pay BRELA incorporation fees only once. You file one single corporate tax return (30% CIT) with the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) using one single TIN. If your software business makes money but your furniture startup loses money in the first year, the loss automatically reduces your overall tax bill. [1, 7] 
* The Risk: If someone sues your advertising division over a copyright issue, the assets of your furniture division and your software code are legally exposed because they belong to the exact same legal "person" (Apex Company Limited).

To protect these names and stay compliant with the Business Registrations and Licensing Agency (BRELA) and the [Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA)](https://www.tra.go.tz/legal/terms-and-conditions), you must follow a specific operational and legal framework.
------------------------------
## 📋 Phase 1: Formalizing the Brands at BRELA
To legally use these names without incorporating four separate companies, you should register them as Business Names tied to your parent company.

* 
* Step 1: The BRELA "Jina la Biashara" Filing
Log into the [BRELA Online Registration System (ORS)](https://ors.brela.go.tz/). Instead of registering a new company, select "Register a Business Name" for each of your four brands.
* Step 2: Assign Ownership to the Parent
When the system asks for the "Proprietor" (Owner) of the business name, do not enter your personal name. Enter Apex Company Limited (and its registration number).
* Step 3: Trademark Protection (Optional but Recommended)
Registering a business name stops someone else from opening a company with that exact name, but it doesn't fully protect your logo or brand identity. To prevent copycats in the competitive furniture and clothing sectors, file for a Trademark under Apex Company Limited's name.
* 

------------------------------
## 🧾 Phase 2: Invoices, Receipts, and TRA Compliance
Because you only have one Tax Identification Number (TIN) and one EFD machine, the TRA requires absolute transparency. If you issue a receipt that says "[Apex Technologies](https://www.apex-jet.com/about-us.html)" but your TRA tax profile only says "[Apex Company Limited](https://www.facebook.com/sibentallc/)," you risk facing penalties for non-disclosure or non-compliance.
## The Legal Footer Rule
Your marketing, shop signs, and logos can boldly display the individual brand names. However, the legal footer on all official documents (Invoices, Quotations, Receipts, and Contracts) must explicitly link back to the parent company.
Example Invoice / Receipt Structure:

[ BRAND LOGO: Apex Technologies ]
Services Rendered: Custom Software Development & E-Commerce Setup
Amount Due: 2,500,000 TZS
------------------------------
Legal Footer:
Apex Technologies is a registered trading style of Apex Company Limited.
TIN: [Your 9-Digit Company TIN] | VRN: [Your VRN if registered for VAT]
Official Address: Plot XX, Lumumba Street, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.

------------------------------
## 📊 Phase 3: Banking & Financial Accounting Structure
Mixing furniture cash flow with software consulting can quickly turn into an accounting nightmare. While you have one legal entity, you must structure your finances cleanly.

* 
* The Bank Setup: Go to your bank (e.g., CRDB, NMB, or Stanbic) with your Apex Company Limited board resolution and BRELA certificates. Request one master corporate account with four sub-accounts or separate checking accounts. Name them:
* Apex Company Limited - Furniture A/C
   * Apex Company Limited - Tech A/C
   * ...and so on.
* Consolidated Tax Return: At the end of the financial year, your accountant will look at the profits and losses of all four branches, merge them into a single financial statement, and file one corporate income tax return for Apex Company Limited.
* 

------------------------------
## ⚠️ Critical Trade-Offs to Keep in Mind

* 
* The Liability Risk: If a customer sues Apex Furnitures because a defective installation caused damage, they are legally suing Apex Company Limited. This means the revenue and assets held by Apex Technologies are exposed to that lawsuit.
* The Business License Multiplication: While you save money on company registration, you cannot buy one single business license to cover everything. You must visit your local Municipal Council (e.g., Ilala, Kinondoni) or relevant ministry and pay for separate business licenses for the furniture workshop, the tech office, the clothing retail shop, and the advertising operations.
